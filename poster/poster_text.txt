01
The business question
Can historical sales help a category manager decide whether to
discount a perishable SKU tomorrow, and by how much,
while limiting loss of predicted sales value?
Public observations, no synthetic labels.
FreshRetailNet-50K (Dingdong): 312 retrospectively
selected store-SKU series; 2,184 final-period daily
cases. Target: observed normalized sales, which can
be limited by stockouts.
TIME ORDER (2024) Train from 4 April | Validate 5 expanding weeks, 22 May–25 June | Benchmark 26 June–2 July
One day ahead, repeatedly: only earlier observed days update history. The final benchmark was already viewed during prior development.

Decision: 0.90 selected (*). Supported candidate passes model agreement and value
floor; mildest near-best sales option.
A rate of 0.90 means 10% off. Alternative-rate predictions are what-if estimates; their outcomes
were not observed.
Screen all 2,184 cases: 1,179 recommend a discount
scenario; 967 require supply review; 38 other cases need
review/abstention. 96.9% of the 1,217 cases without a
recent-stockout flag still suggest discounting. This high rate
needs further investigation.
Transfer stress check: 700 cases across 93 new stores / 16
other cities. Proposed vs. original blend: MAE 0.2890 vs.
0.2884; RMSE 0.4886 vs. 0.4710. The improvement does not
transfer here. We kept the model frozen; no retuning.
Limits: discounts are observational; stockouts do not reveal inventory
age or waste. Rate × sales is a proxy, not measured revenue or profit.
Pricing gains require intervention evidence.
